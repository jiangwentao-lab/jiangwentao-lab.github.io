We are a multidisciplinary research group dedicated to **liver transplantation, organ preservation, liver cancer, and translational medicine**, bridging clinical practice with experimental and emerging technologies.

## Research Areas

### Liver Transplantation
Clinical and translational research to improve graft function and outcomes after liver transplantation.

### Organ Preservation & Machine Perfusion
Machine perfusion for organ preservation, viability assessment, therapeutic delivery, and graft reconditioning.

### Donor Optimization & Graft Reconditioning
Metabolic and pharmacological strategies to improve steatotic and marginal donor livers.

### Liver Cancer & Transplant Oncology
Tumor biology, recurrence prediction, precision treatment, and transplantation for hepatic malignancies.

### Organoids & Translational Medicine
Patient-derived organoids integrated with multi-omics, spatial biology, advanced imaging, and artificial intelligence.

## Our Mission

To translate scientific discoveries and emerging technologies into better strategies for **organ preservation, transplantation, and liver cancer treatment**.
