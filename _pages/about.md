The **Jiang Laboratory** is dedicated to advancing basic, translational, and clinical research in liver transplantation and liver diseases. Our research focuses on organ preservation and machine perfusion, donor organ assessment and optimization, marginal and steatotic donor livers, liver cancer and transplant oncology, and patient-derived organoid models. By integrating experimental models, multi-omics, spatial biology, advanced imaging, and artificial intelligence, we aim to develop new strategies for organ transplantation and the treatment of liver diseases.
