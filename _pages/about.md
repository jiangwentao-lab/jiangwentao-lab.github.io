The **Jiang Laboratory** conducts basic, translational, and clinical research in liver transplantation and liver diseases. Our work focuses on organ preservation and machine perfusion, donor organ assessment and optimization, marginal and steatotic liver grafts, liver cancer and transplant oncology, as well as patient-derived organoid models. By integrating experimental models, multi-omics, spatial biology, advanced imaging, and artificial intelligence, we aim to develop new strategies for organ transplantation and the treatment of liver diseases.

**Translational Research Center for Medical Devices in Organ Transplantation**  
**Tianjin Key Laboratory of Liver Cancer**  
Tianjin First Central Hospital  
Nankai University / Tianjin Medical University  
Tianjin, China
