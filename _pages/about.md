The **Jiang Laboratory** is dedicated to advancing basic, translational, and clinical research in liver transplantation and liver diseases. Our research focuses on organ preservation and machine perfusion, donor organ assessment and optimization, and strategies to improve the utilization of marginal and steatotic donor livers. We also investigate liver cancer and transplant oncology, with particular interests in tumor biology, recurrence prediction, and personalized treatment. By integrating patient-derived organoids, experimental models, multi-omics, spatial biology, advanced imaging, and artificial intelligence, we aim to translate scientific discoveries into new approaches for organ transplantation and the treatment of liver diseases.
