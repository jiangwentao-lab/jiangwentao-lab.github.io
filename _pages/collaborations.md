Our laboratory maintains active collaborations with clinicians, scientists, and research teams across institutions in China and internationally. These collaborations bring together expertise in organ transplantation, machine perfusion, transplant oncology, organoid models, bioinformatics, and artificial intelligence to facilitate multidisciplinary and translational research.

We welcome new collaborations with researchers, clinicians, and industry partners who share our scientific interests.
